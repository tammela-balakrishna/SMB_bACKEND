Privacy Policy

SMU Bike Spare Parts ("SMU", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the SMU mobile application and related services.

By using the SMU application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SMU, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Authentication information
  • Firebase authentication identifier associated with your account

Delivery Information

When you place an order, we collect information necessary to process and deliver your order, including:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • City, state, and postal information where provided

Vehicle Information

SMU allows customers to save vehicle information to help provide relevant products and services. This may include:

  • Vehicle brand
  • Vehicle model
  • Variant
  • Vehicle year

Order Information

  • Products ordered
  • Quantity and order details
  • Order status
  • Order history
  • Delivery information
  • Cash-on-delivery information

Information We Do Not Collect

SMU does not require or collect:

  • Profile photos
  • GPS/device location
  • Card details
  • UPI details
  • Bank account details

2. Google Sign-In

SMU provides Google Sign-In for customer authentication.

When you sign in with Google, we may receive information such as your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google/Firebase authentication identifier

We use this information to authenticate your account and provide SMU services.

SMU does not receive or store your Google account password.

3. Firebase Authentication

SMU uses Firebase Authentication to securely authenticate customers.

Firebase authentication information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate your SMU account
  • Secure your account
  • Associate your authenticated identity with your SMU account
  • Prevent unauthorized access

4. OTP Verification

SMU uses email-based OTP verification for:

  • New account registration
  • Password reset

OTP is not required for normal login when using the applicable authentication method.

OTP codes are temporary and should never be shared with anyone.

5. Email Services

SMU uses Resend to send transactional emails, including OTP and account-related emails.

Your email address and information necessary to deliver these emails may be processed by Resend.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts
  • Authenticate customers
  • Verify email addresses
  • Reset passwords
  • Process customer orders
  • Arrange order delivery
  • Contact customers regarding orders
  • Maintain order history
  • Provide customer support
  • Provide relevant vehicle/product functionality
  • Send necessary push notifications
  • Protect the security of the application
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized activity
  • Improve our services

7. Cash on Delivery

SMU currently provides Cash on Delivery (COD) as the payment method.

SMU does not collect or store customers' credit/debit card information, UPI credentials, or bank account credentials.

8. Push Notifications

SMU may send push notifications to provide information such as:

  • Order updates
  • Order status
  • Account-related notifications
  • Important service notifications

You may control notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Location Information

SMU does not request or collect precise GPS/device location information from customers.

Delivery addresses are provided by customers when required for order fulfillment.

10. Information Sharing

SMU does not sell or rent customers' personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers when necessary to operate SMU, including services used for:

  • Authentication
  • Email delivery
  • Database hosting
  • Application hosting
  • Image/media services where applicable
  • Technical infrastructure

These providers process information as necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, governmental request, or when necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of SMU, our customers, or others.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should keep their passwords and OTPs confidential and should never share them with others.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Maintain customer accounts
  • Process and maintain order records
  • Provide customer support
  • Meet applicable legal requirements
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
